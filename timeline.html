Concert Timeline

This page is a list of a lot of the concerts I’ve gone to. I didn’t include all of them, since some were just free concerts, or we just went because we got tickets sent to us (The Rave does this a lot). I wanted to focus on the more important concerts/bands for this! Also, some of them have some fun facts to them, about the concert itself or fun experiences!

Sort By Band -->

06/25/14 Neon Trees

Venue: Summerfest

Fun Fact: This my first concert ever! They were my favorite band at the time so it was so fun! We weren’t even going to go see them that day, but the Big Bang Fireworks got canceled because of fog, so we were able to see them instead!

02/15/18 Starset

Openers: Palisades, Grabbitz, Year Of The Locust

Venue: The Rave/Eagles Ballroom

Fun Fact: This was my first time ever going to The Rave. I didn’t know a single song of theirs at the time, and I wasn’t even supposed to go, but my sisters friend canceled so I went. It was so fun and probably one of the coolest concerts that I went to, even though I was also sick that day.
