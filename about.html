About

Sam S.

sscharpf@uwm.edu

I love going to concerts, and I spend a lot of my time going to them. I have gone to about 40 concerts in my life! It’s a great thing to do with my sister or with friends, and I love supporting my favorite bands. I also even worked at The Rave for a little bit, just based on how much I love concerts. I knew that I wanted to focus on concerts for this project, because I knew that I would have a lot of information on it, and it would be a fun website to make.

I want people to come to this site to find out more about me and my concert experiences and my favorite bands. But also, I want people that just want to learn about more bands, to have a good starting place in a bunch of different genres!
