Concert Wishlist

This page contains a list of the bands that I want to see for the first time and bands that I really want to see again. I obviously want to see all of these bands again, I’m always up for concerts, but these bands (that I’ve seen before) are ones that I especially really want to see again. Some of the bands listed, are going to be nearly impossible for me to see, especially soon, so I’m also going to list that for those bands!

AJR

Why I want to see them: Their shows are so chaotic in such a fun and interesting way, that it just has to be a blast to go to one.

Why I probably wont: The tickets are so expensive, and I would rather pay for a lot more cheaper concerts than one concert.

All Time Low

Why I want to see them again: They have been one of my favorite bands for a really long time and their shows are always so good! The last time I saw them was probably my second favorite concert ever, and that’s saying a lot, based on how many I’ve been to!

Ax and the Hatchetmen

Why I want to see them again: They have been one of my favorite bands recently, I want to see them this November, and I hope I can. Their shows are always so good, and they always play the best songs.

BOYS LIKE GIRLS

Why I want to see them: I love so many of their songs, and when I’m listening to a random mix, I always like their songs, even when I don’t realize its them.

Why I probably wont: The tickets are so expensive, and I would rather pay for a lot more cheaper concerts than one concert. Also, I don’t think they tour that often. Also, they aren’t really one of my favorites, so It’s not really worth it.

Bring Me The Horizon

Why I want to see them again: I only saw them the once, and it was such a unique experience, and I didn’t know a lot of their songs at the time, so it would be cool to see them again.

Why I probably wont: They are from the UK, so they aren’t here very often.

Grayscale

Why I want to see them again: I have been listening to them a lot recently, and haven’t really seen them when I was a true fan of theirs.

Why I probably wont: They usually open for other bands, and it’s not really worth it to go to it if I don’t care about/know the other bands.

OneRepublic

Why I want to see them: I love so many of their songs, and when I’m listening to a random mix, I always like their songs, even when I don’t realize its them.

Why I probably wont: The tickets are so expensive, and I would rather pay for a lot more cheaper concerts than one concert. Also, I don’t think they tour that often. Also, they aren’t really one of my favorites, so It’s not really worth it.

Owl City

Why I want to see them: Owl City was one of the very first bands I ever listened to. ‘Fireflies’ will always be an amazing song to me and will always be in my playlist.

Why I probably wont: He kind of just sits at a piano the whole time, so I feel like it might be a bit boring, compared to other bands.

The Script

Why I want to see them: I have loved ‘Breakeven’ and ‘Hall of Fame’ for most of my life, so I just really want to hear those songs live.

Why I probably wont: They are from Ireland, so they aren’t here very often, and also their tickets are pretty expensive too.

Set It Off

Why I want to see them again: This is my favorite band! Their concerts are always top tier, and I know that I will see them so many more times in my life.

Starset

Why I want to see them again: When I did see them, they were really good, but I didn’t know any of their songs and I was very sick, so I want to actually be able to enjoy the show next time. They also have some of the coolest sets/lights/lasers and outfits!

Why I probably wont: They don’t visit The Rave very often, and they have some of the best priced tickets, and are also super close. They visit Chicago often, but I think that’s too long of a drive.

State Champs

Why I want to see them again: When I had a chance to see them, we were busy stuck in a food truck line, so I really only saw like two songs.

Why I probably wont: They don’t come around here often, and they were just at Summerfest, but I was busy that day, so I just have really bad luck with them!

Twenty One Pilots

Why I want to see them: They do a lot of costume changes and a lot of other things between songs, so their show would be so fun.

Why I probably wont: The tickets are so expensive, and I would rather pay for a lot more cheaper concerts than one concert.

M H
